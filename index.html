I didn't start as a "website guy." I actually wanted to become a doctor. Life took me in a different direction, and instead of treating patients, I started diagnosing and fixing websites.

Things You're Probably Thinking Right Now

Getting some leads isn't the question. The question is how many you're losing. A funnel converting at 1.5% when it should be at 4% isn't "working" - it's leaking revenue you've already earned. You just can't see the leads leave.
A web designer asks you what you want it to look like. I start by asking why prospects aren't booking, then build the answer to that question. Positioning strategy, buyer psychology, and conversion architecture come before design. Most designers skip that entirely and hand you something pretty that still doesn't convert.
You can and you'd probably produce something that looks reasonable. What you won't produce is a system where every word, every section, and every CTA is engineered against one specific goal: getting a qualified prospect to book a call. Templates are built for everyone, which means they're optimised for no one.
